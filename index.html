About Me

I'm a passionate and versatile software developer with a deep love for building, learning, and sharing what I know. My experience spans everything from developing complex graphical programs in C++ using raylib to designing real-time audio waveform visualization and playback software of my own. I thrive in both collaborative and independent environments, and I enjoy tackling new challenges across all layers of development.

Beyond software, I’m deeply involved in my campus tech community. As Vice President of multiple student coding clubs, I mentor peers on personal and academic projects, organize events, and work closely with faculty to improve club engagement and create opportunities for students to grow. I also help lead summer coding and electronics camps, where I’ve developed custom software tools to streamline instruction and taught students to build circuits on Raspberry Pi and Le Potato boards. My work has earned recognition — I’ve won a cybersecurity competition and placed first in an ICPC qualifying round with my team.

I bring a strong foundation in Linux, terminal-based workflows, and tools like Vim, tmux, Bash, and C++, which I often use to create automation scripts and productivity tools for both personal and academic use. My background also extends into electronics and hardware prototyping, where I’ve designed and built projects with Arduino and other embedded systems.

Outside of tech, I’m a competitive pool player and an avid photographer — I’ve had professionally edited photos published in print — and I bring the same creative drive and eye for detail from those pursuits into my software projects. Originally from Montana and now based in California, I strive to grow every day, push my boundaries, and build things that make an impact.
